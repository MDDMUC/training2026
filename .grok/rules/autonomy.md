# Autonomy (Training 2026)

On this project, default to autonomous continuation.

- Do not end with "tell me what to build next." Pick the next-best item from the open list and do it.
- Do not ask between obvious sub-steps ("should I also wire it up?" — yes).
- Confirm only for genuinely destructive or hard-to-reverse work (deleting committed code, dropping tables, changing the stack, force-pushing, pushing to `main`).
- "Keep going" or silence is implicit approval to continue through the open list.

Call out trade-offs as they appear. Do not gate further work on an answer unless it is a real decision.

Keep end-of-response summaries tight: what changed, what is still open.
